Topic Tracker for YouTube

See how much time you actually spend on YouTube, sorted into topics, with daily goals on a calendar.

Everything stays in your browser. There is no server, no account and no analytics. Read the privacy policy.

What it does

  • Topics: time per category, worked out automatically
  • Calendar: set a daily goal and watch the ring fill; streaks included
  • Review: see the evidence behind every guess, and correct it

Corrections apply to your whole history, not just future videos. Pin a channel to a topic once and every video from it follows, past and future.
